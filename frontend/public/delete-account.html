Account & Data Deletion

If you would like to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data from the St. Paul's High School SKLM ERP, please contact the school administration.

What data gets deleted?
Requesting account deletion will permanently remove your profile information, contact details, uploaded photos, and login credentials from our systems.

Please click the button below to email the school administration with your deletion request. Be sure to include your name and the phone number/email associated with your account.
