Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the St. Paul's High School SKLM ERP application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data Collection

Our app collects information necessary for school administration, including student profiles, academic records, attendance, and communication logs. We also request access to your device's camera and photo library strictly for uploading profile pictures and submitting assignments or documents.

3. Data Usage

The collected data is used exclusively for educational and administrative purposes within St. Paul's High School. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the school administration.
